ELEGANCE WITHOUT FORMALITY
Sichuan Alley began with a quiet vision: to create the kind of place we would always want to return to. A place where familiar dishes carry the memories of where we come from, while making room for new stories to begin. Our journey began in Chengdu. Today, that journey continues across the kitchens of Southwest China — through Sichuan's layered seasonings, Yunnan's mushrooms and herbs, and Guizhou's remarkable traditions of fermentation and sourness. Recipes travel. Families evolve. Kitchens grow with every season. Our food is rooted in tradition, yet never limited by it.
FROM CHENGDU, INTO THE WORLD
OUR SPACE

Tables, chairs, tiles, glassware and light are composed as one scene - considered, harmonious and welcoming rather than formal.
The wider journey from Chengdu into international cities has unfolded over a decade. The team behind Sichuan Alley began its Singapore chapter in 2016. Sichuan Alley opened in 2019 and later continued to the United States. What changes is the city around us. What remains is the food: authentic flavours from Southwest China, cooked with the memory, instinct and care carried from home.
The room is not a backdrop to the meal. It is part of how Sichuan Alley is experienced.
